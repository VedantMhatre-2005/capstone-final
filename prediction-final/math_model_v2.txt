Dataset  1.  Node  Features  (  and  )  These  provide  the  real-time  status  of  traffic  at  specific  junctions  or  sensor  locations.  
●  Traffic  Flow/Volume:  Number  of  vehicles  passing  the  node  per  time  interval.  ●  Average  Speed:  Mean  velocity  of  vehicles  at  the  node  at  time  .  ●  Lane  Occupancy:  Percentage  of  time  the  sensor  is  occupied,  indicating  density.  ●  Queue  Length:  Number  of  vehicles  or  length  of  the  backup  at  the  intersection.  ●  Intersection  Type:  Functional  characteristics  (e.g.,  signalized,  roundabout,  or  
priority-based).

 ●  Speed  Limit:  Regulatory  maximum  speed  for  the  segment.  ●  Lane  Count:  Number  of  available  lanes  for  traffic  flow.  ●  Incident  Flags:  Binary  indicators  for  active  accidents  or  road  construction.  
3.  Weather  Features  (  and  )  Weather  acts  as  a  dynamic  modulator  for  the  node  embeddings  and  the  quantum  update  stage.  
●  Precipitation  Intensity:  Rainfall  rates  (mm/hr).  ●  Visibility:  Distance  of  clear  sight  (meters),  affected  by  fog  or  haze.  ●  Temperature:  Ambient  temperature  ( C),  impacting  road  surface  conditions.

proximity.
 ●  Day  of  Week:  Indicator  for  weekday  vs.  weekend  patterns.  ●  Holiday  Flag:  Binary  indicator  for  public  holidays  or  school  breaks.  ●  Special  Events:  Flags  for  nearby  major  social  gatherings  (concerts,  sporting  events).
